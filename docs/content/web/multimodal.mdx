title: Multimodal
description: Attach images, PDFs, structures, audio, and video with @paths or clipboard paste.
---

Attach files to any message using `@` in the composer, drag-and-drop, or `Cmd+V` / `Ctrl+V` clipboard paste.

## Supported Inputs

- **Images (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.webp`, `.gif`, `.svg`)**: Sent directly to vision-capable cloud and local models. Pasting an image from your clipboard saves it under the session's `uploads/` folder and inserts an attachment chip.
- **Documents & Data (`.pdf`, `.csv`, `.tsv`, `.json`, `.md`, `.ipynb`)**: Native PDF support on Gemini, Claude, and OpenAI models, plus direct workspace path access for sandbox Python/bash tools.
- **Audio & Video**: Supported natively by Gemini models and previewable inline in the Artifacts panel.
- **Molecular Structures (`.pdb`, `.cif`, `.sdf`, `.fasta`)**: Automatically recognized by scientific tools and rendered in the 3D structure viewer.
