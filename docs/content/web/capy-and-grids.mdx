**Capy** is Sandwalk's built-in meta-agent (`⌃⌥C` or the Capy button at the bottom-left of the sidebar). Unlike a regular session agent that works inside a single chat, Capy has full awareness of your active sessions, projects, Specialists, Memory/Wiki/Omnigraph, external coding CLIs, and benchmarking harness.

## Parallel Multi-Agent Split Grids

Ask Capy to split a complex question across multiple sessions or Specialist personas at once:

- *"Spawn a 2-session split grid with Nova and Soren to debate oral GLP-1R titration vs. hERG safety."*
- *"Create 4 parallel chats investigating KRAS G12D resistance mechanisms across different models."*
- *"Summarize session #abc123 and seed a new chat with Otium from its findings."*

## What Capy Can Do

- **Session & Grid Orchestration**: `capy.spawn_sessions` (with `specialists="Nova,Soren"`), `capy.open_split`, `capy.seed_session`, `capy.read_session`, `capy.summarize_session`.
- **Memory, Wiki & Skill Stewardship**: `capy.memory_manage`, `capy.memory_search`, `capy.wiki_digest`, `capy.distill_session`, and `capy.create_skill_from_memory`.
- **External Coding Agents**: Import sessions from or hand off chats to **Claude Code**, **Codex**, **OpenCode**, **fx**, and **Hermes**.
- **Benchmarking & Diagnostics**: Launch `sandwalk eval` suites (`capy.bench_launch`), inspect results (`capy.bench_inspect`), or run `capy.doctor`.
