**Specialists** (`/dashboard/specialists`) bundle a cognitive style (`PRIORS.md`), a default model, an allowlist of skills, tools, and MCP servers, and a dedicated **Specialist Brain** (`MEMORY.md` + Omnigraph ontology lens) into a reusable profile you can pick in the Composer (`+ → Specialist`), summon via **Capy**, or select in the CLI (`/specialist`).

## Starter Personas & Domain Specialists

Sandwalk seeds a roster of starter Specialists—illustrated with 19th-century natural history woodcut animal portraits—alongside domain-specific scientific specialists. **These are starter defaults**: you can edit any of them or click **`+ New specialist`** in the top-right header to create your own.

| Starter Persona | Role / Archetype | Default Ontology Lens | Cognitive Priors (`PRIORS.md`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Otium** (Capybara) | `Relaxed` | `06 Second brain` / `05 Biomed research` | Calm, unhurried contemplative thinker. De-escalates complexity, cuts busywork, finds the simplest durable solution, and distills messy sessions into clean Living Wiki notes. |
| **Soren** (Owl) | `Skeptical Reviewer` | `02 Pharma intel` | Methodological red-teamer. Hunts for confounders, batch effects, off-target liabilities, and contradicted assumptions before approving a conclusion. |
| **Milo** (Badger) | `Project Manager` | `03 Context graph` | Structured execution lead. Turns ambiguous goals into clear milestones, dependencies, risk registers, and accountable action items. |
| **Nova** (Fox) | `Dreamer` | `05 Biomed research` | Divergent hypothesis generator. Connects cross-disciplinary mechanisms, proposes unconventional experiments, and explores adjacent possibilities. |
| **Atlas** (Wolf) | `Ambitious Generalist` | `01 Industry intel` | High-agency polymath. Synthesizes across code, biology, systems architecture, and strategy to ship end-to-end artifacts fast. |

Sandwalk also ships with scientific Specialists (**Target Discovery**, **Protein Designer**, **Medicinal Chemist**, **Single-cell Analyst**, **Genomics Analyst**, **Clinical Trial Strategist**, **Literature Synthesizer**, and **Fast Computational Scientist**).

## Creating a Custom Specialist (`+ New specialist`)

<div className="not-prose my-6 overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-fd-border">
  <img
    src="/images/specialists-new.png"
    alt="Create Specialist editor: avatar picker, name, slug, instructions, skills, and Core/Extra tools allowlist"
    className="w-full"
  />
</div>

1. Open **Dashboard → Specialists** and click **`+ New specialist`** in the top-right header.
2. **Identity & Appearance**:
   - Click the avatar circle to choose from the built-in woodcut animal portraits, icons, or color accents.
   - Enter a **Name**, optional **Slug**, short **Description** (shown in pickers), and **Instructions** (appended to the system prompt when this Specialist is active).
3. **Skills, Tools & MCP Allowlists**:
   - **Skills**: Attach specific installed or crystallized skills (`Add a skill...`).
   - **Tools**: Toggle which **Core** and **Extra** tools this Specialist can call (keeping unrelated domain tools off so the prompt budget stays fast and focused).
   - **MCPs**: Scope which MCP servers are active for this Specialist.
4. Click **Create specialist**.

## Using the Profile Inspector Drawer

Click any card in **Dashboard → Specialists** to open the right-side **Profile Inspector Drawer**:

- **Info**: Edit `Cognitive Priors & Biases (PRIORS.md)`, role label, custom avatar URL, and active Omnigraph Cookbook lens (`01–06`) inline.
- **Runtime**: Set a dedicated default model and provider for this Specialist.
- **Skills**: Inspect and manage attached skills.
- **Memories (`specialist:<id>`)**: View and teach durable rules directly to this Specialist's private brain, or click **Explore Brain & Ontology Graph** to open its scoped Knowledge Graph in **[Memory & Wiki](/memory)**.
- **Quick Start Chat (`▶`)**: Click the play button on any card (or in the drawer header) to immediately launch a chat with that Specialist selected in the Composer toolbar (`[Avatar] Otium ×`).
